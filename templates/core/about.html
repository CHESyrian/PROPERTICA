{% trans "About Propertica" %}

Propertica is a real estate listings marketplace where property owners, agents, and agencies list homes, land, and commercial properties for sale — and buyers search, filter, and inquire in one place.

Our mission

Buyers often have to check multiple fragmented sources to find relevant listings. Sellers and agents need a simple way to publish listings that reach a wide audience. We centralize supply and demand into one searchable, trustworthy marketplace.

Who it’s for

  • Buyers — powerful search and filters, accurate details, easy contact with sellers.
  • Private sellers — simple listing creation and lead management.
  • Agents & agencies — multi-listing tools, branded profiles, and inbox for inquiries.

What’s in v1

Sale listings only (houses, apartments, land, commercial). Free plan for all account types. Messaging between buyers and sellers. More features — including paid plans and advanced search — are on the roadmap.
